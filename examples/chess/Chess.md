# Chess

A game of chess for two players at one screen (or taking turns, if the page is synced between you), or against the computer: set **engine** to `white`, `black` or `both`, and **level** from 1 (gentle) to 8 (full strength).

1. Press **New game** to set up the board (it is only a page of text until then).
2. The player to move presses **Play move** and types the move (`e4`, `Nf3`, `exd5`, `O-O`, or by squares: `e2e4`, `e7e8=Q`).
3. The board is redrawn, and the move is added to the list.
